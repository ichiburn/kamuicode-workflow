キジトラ猫の自然で愛らしい日常的な動作を表現した、癒し効果の高い短尺動画コンセプト。視聴者にリラックス感と温かさを提供することを目的としています。

```
Generate a peaceful and natural short video of the brown tabby cat featuring gentle slow blinking, subtle head tilts with curious expressions, soft breathing movements in the chest area, delicate fur texture movements in warm lighting, maintaining the serene and charming atmosphere throughout a 3-5 second loop perfect for relaxation and healing content, with smooth transitions optimized for Vidu Q1 video generation.
```
